About the Artist

Hello, I'm Elena.

For over twelve years I've explored the space where color meets emotion. My work blends traditional oil painting with modern digital illustration, drawing from quiet mornings, city textures, and the natural world. Every piece begins as a feeling before it becomes a form.

My mission: to create art that slows people down — pieces that invite a second look and a longer breath.
